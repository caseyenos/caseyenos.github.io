My Projects

Patient Database System 2026

MySQL 8.0  ·  Open Source  ·  Healthcare Data
View on GitHub

Are you an aspiring data analyst or healthcare informatics professional looking to sharpen your SQL skills with realistic data? I built this for you. The Patient Database System 2026 is a fully normalized, production-style MySQL database populated with 1,000 simulated patient records across 13 interconnected tables — covering patients, physicians, diagnoses, prescriptions, admissions, lab results, vitals, insurance, and more.

Every record is grounded in real healthcare concepts: ICD-10-CM diagnosis codes, a 40-medication formulary, PACS-style imaging workflow data, and referential integrity throughout. The schema includes 5 analytical views, 3 business-rule triggers, and 4 stored procedures — giving you a realistic environment to practice the kinds of queries and data challenges you'll face in actual healthcare data roles.

Whether you're learning SQL joins, exploring healthcare data normalization, or preparing for a role in clinical data administration or PACS analytics, this dataset is designed to give you hands-on experience with the real structure of hospital data. Free to use, clone, and explore.

13 Normalized Tables Fully relational schema in Third Normal Form (3NF)
1,000 Patient Records Simulated patients with demographics, history & insurance
15,000+ Total Dataset Rows Spread across all interconnected tables
5 Analytical Views Prebuilt SQL views for common reporting queries
ICD-10 Diagnosis Coding Real ICD-10-CM codes across 30 diagnosis categories
4 Stored Procedures Patient profiles, search, dept stats & reporting
MySQL Database Engine 8.0+ with triggers, foreign keys & generated columns
Free Open Source Clone, study, and build on it — no restrictions
